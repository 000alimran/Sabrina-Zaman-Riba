( ০৯ ) — FAQ

Questions, answered

What brands ask before the first booking.

How far ahead should we book?

Campaigns and editorials: two weeks is comfortable. Bridal season and fashion weeks book out earlier — enquire a month ahead.

Do you travel for shoots?

Yes — recent work includes an international shoot in Kolkata, and the current fashion film is headed to Europe. Travel and stay on the client.

Is styling included?

Styling direction is available on any booking; a full styling credit (as with Merlot) is scoped separately in the brief.

Do you choreograph shows you're not walking in?

Yes. Show choreography and ramp direction can be booked on their own.

How do payments work?

Advance on confirmation, balance on delivery. Rate card and terms come with the first reply.

Still curious? Ask directly — serious briefs get a reply within 48 hours.
