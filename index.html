About

Sabrina Zaman Riba has been one of the familiar faces of Bangladesh's fashion industry for more than a decade — walking its biggest ramps, fronting campaigns and shaping editorials.

The work no longer stops at the end of the catwalk. Riba styles the shoots she appears in, choreographs shows, and recently co-directed a music video shot between Dhaka and Kolkata. Her latest project — a fashion film with a European Fashion Film Award–winning director — is headed for festivals in Europe and America.

She takes on runway, editorial, bridal and commercial work, in Bangladesh and abroad.
